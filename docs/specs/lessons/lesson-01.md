# Lesson 1 quote set: Consciousness Is the Only Reality

Status: **draft, not approved**. It is blocked on the rights decision in #43 and on the review gates in `../lessons.md`. A dev sample only, never for production.

Source: Neville Goddard, *Five Lessons*, Lesson 1, "Consciousness Is the Only Reality" (Los Angeles, 1948), as transcribed at https://realneville.com/pdf/lesson1.pdf. The PDF is 10 pages, with no copyright notice and no transcriber credit. Page numbers are pages of that PDF.

Quotes are Neville's exact words from the PDF, with nothing added. The PDF prints some words in capitals for emphasis (FEELING, HERE, NOW). The spoken text below uses normal case and keeps every word. One fix: "mans" in quote 7 is printed that way in the PDF and is spoken as "man's".

## Quote set (ranked strongest first)

Picked for recall and for use the same day, not to cover the whole lesson. The recorder reads rows 1 to 6 for the sample.

| # | Page | Big idea | Quote (spoken text) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9 | Make elsewhere here, now | You do not visualize yourself at a distance in point of space and at a distance in point of time being congratulated on your good fortune. Instead, you make elsewhere here and the future now. |
| 2 | 3 | Assumptions harden into fact | Man, by assuming the feeling of his wish fulfilled, alters his future in harmony with his assumption, for, assumptions though false, if sustained, will harden into fact. |
| 3 | 9 | Feel it, don't watch it | The difference between feeling yourself in action, here and now, and visualizing yourself in action, as though you were on a motion-picture screen, is the difference between success and failure. |
| 4 | 9 | Imagine what follows fulfillment | It is important always to remember that the proposed action must be one which follows the fulfillment of your desire, one which implies fulfillment. |
| 5 | 9 | One act, repeated until real | Experience has taught me to restrict the imaginary action which implies fulfillment of the desire, to condense the idea into a single act, and to re-enact it over and over again until it has the feeling of reality. |
| 6 | 9–10 | Drowsy "thank you" lullaby | A most effective way to embody a desire is to assume the feeling of the wish fulfilled and then, in a relaxed and drowsy state, repeat over and over again like a lullaby, any short phrase which implies fulfillment of your desire, such as, thank you, thank you, thank you. |
| 7 | 3 | Self-concept is the cornerstone | The corner-stone on which all things are based is man's concept of himself. |
| 8 | 6 | Don't fight what is | You do not send your visible world hunting, as so many people do, by denial. By saying it does not exist you make it all the more real. |

Reserve, the whole method in one quote (p. 5): "Know that your consciousness is the only reality. Then know what you want to be. Then assume the feeling of being that which you want to be, and remain faithful to your assumption, living and acting on your conviction."

Mode notes:
